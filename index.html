How it is built

Python loads the bars, writes the signals, and fills them from ticks. The same rules sit in MQL5. Both sides use one governor: two slots, a cap on open risk, a daily halt, and a weekly lock. Gold is the market I have worked. GER40, US500, and EURUSD are in the tester.

If a sleeve only works on the window it was fitted on, it does not get a seat. Session RSI made money in 2026 and lost money from 2023 through 2026, so it stays out of the core book.
