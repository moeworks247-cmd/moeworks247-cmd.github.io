Why this developer

A gold desk does not hire the person with the prettiest equity curve. It hires the person who can rank many advisors on one instrument, put risk on the ones with expectancy, and refuse the ones that only worked in the window they were tuned on.

The common comparison is FTMO XAUUSD from 1 January 2026 to 28 September 2026, about 38.6 weeks, scored at 1.5% risk per trade. Seventeen books were run in the same Python research engine. Five belong in a funded portfolio. Three more printed a profit and still do not get capital.

Funded portfolio

Two core advisors carry the book. Three smaller sleeves add a different entry and are sized so a cold streak cannot dominate the day. Weights below are a risk committee allocation built from the sleeve results. They are not a combined backtest, and this page does not invent an equity curve for the blend.

How the book is run

  1. Two positions at most. One position per advisor.
  2. Open risk cap of 1.50%. If Secret Sauce and Aurum both signal, size is cut so the pair cannot put 1.80% on at once.
  3. No new trades after the account is up 2.5% on the day.
  4. Hard daily loss at 4% of the starting balance.
  5. If drawdown from the start reaches 6%, Midpoint, Asian Breakout, and Volatility Expansion turn off. Only the two core advisors may still trade.
  6. Priority when signals collide: Secret Sauce, then Aurum, then Midpoint, then Asian Breakout, then Volatility Expansion.

A path that was actually combined

Secret Sauce, Aurum, and a session RSI filler were run together from 1 July 2026 to 28 September 2026. Two slots, daily loss gate, weekly lock. Result: equity +24.38%, max drawdown 5.9%, win rate 50.9%, expectancy +0.312 R, profit factor 1.615, Sharpe 4.14, 4.23 trades per week.

Sleeve contribution inside that path: Secret Sauce +14.41% on 8 trades, Aurum +5.64% on 11 trades, session RSI +2.91% on 36 trades. The cores did the work. The filler supplied frequency.

A challenge test of Secret Sauce, Aurum, Session RSI, and Volatility Expansion, with core risk above filler risk, passed a +10% target in 5 of 9 monthly starts in 2026 and breached on 0 of the rolling 30 day windows. The same four sleeves at a flat 1.5% each still passed 48.7% of those windows and breached 38.5% of them. That gap is the quant development. Return did not come from raising risk until the challenge broke. Session RSI stays out of the funded seat because the longer tape loses money.
