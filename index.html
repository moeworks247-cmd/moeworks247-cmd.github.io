Research to execution system

The process is engineered. The live blotter is not attached.

This is the control path that exists in the code. Each box is a module or a gate, not a performance claim.

Data Instrument registry and OHLC loaders. Tick paths for gold. XAUUSD is the researched book. GER40, US500, and EURUSD are wired, not the commodity case.
Signal Rule books with explicit stops, targets, and hold limits. Ranked on one tape so sleeves can be compared.
Fill model strategy_tester turns bar signals into real-tick or synthetic fills, then expectancy and profit factor.
Validation In sample, out of sample, a fixed forward window, and a rolling challenge. A sleeve that flips sign is refused.
Risk gate IdealCombo_FTMO.mqh: two slots, package cap, daily halt, weekly lock, challenge lock. Mode A stops at the target. Mode B keeps the gates.
Execution MQL5 advisors for Secret Sauce, Aurum, and the other sleeves, sharing that governor. Python fills are not broker fills.
Monitor Reports record trades, weekly and monthly paths, breach counts, and why a sleeve was cut. There is no live account monitor in the repository.
Feedback Forward windows and the 3.6 year tape are the iteration loop. Session RSI is the example of a 2026 profit that does not survive the longer sample.

Research to software

One problem, carried through.

The gold book is the evidence that an idea was not left as a note. Each stage below is something this repository actually contains.

1. Observation Session behaviour, range reclaim, compression, and liquidity sweep ideas on gold bars.
2. Model Rule books in Python, with explicit stops, targets, and holding limits. No black box claimed.
3. Simulation Bar signals repath’d on ticks. In sample, forward, and rolling challenge windows.
4. Risk Sleeve risk, open risk cap, daily halt, and a lock once a challenge target is reached.
5. Implementation IdealCombo advisors on M30 and M15, plus a shared IdealCombo_FTMO.mqh governor.
6. Performance Ranked sleeves, a three month combined path, and a written refusal of the sleeves that fail the longer tape.
7. Limits The engine records that a Python R path is not a broker fill. That limit stays visible.
8. What is absent No Rust or C++ runtime, no exchange gateway, and no measured production latency in this repository.

Risk sizing

Same pass rate, different breach

A sized challenge book of Secret Sauce, Aurum, session RSI, and volatility expansion passed 48.7% of rolling 30 day starts and breached 0%. Flat 1.5% on those sleeves also passed 48.7% and breached 38.5%. Five of nine monthly 2026 starts reached +10% without a breach under the sized rules.

Engineering

The tester is a product, not a script pile.

Market data

OHLC loaders and a registry for XAUUSD, GER40, US500, and EURUSD. Tick paths for gold. GER40, US500, and EURUSD are wired into the tester. They are not the researched commodity book, and they are not claimed as such.

Simulation

Signals on bars, fills from real ticks or a synthetic path, then expectancy, profit factor, and win rate. Forward mode splits in sample, out of sample, a fixed forward window, and a rolling challenge.

Production shape

MQL5 advisors share magic numbers, slot limits, a daily halt, a weekly profit lock, and a challenge lock. Mode A stops at the challenge target. Mode B keeps the risk gates and drops the account lock.
